# Hedging Bitcoin with Its Own Fear Gauge

**Evidence from the BVIV perpetual on volatility forecasts, funding costs and 15-minute VWAP timing**

📄 **Paper:** [`paper/main.pdf`](paper/main.pdf) (8 pages including references)

A perpetual future on the Volmex **BVIV** index (30-day BTC implied volatility) has traded on Bitfinex since April 2024. This repository uses its full record to ask two questions:

1. How should BTC volatility be forecast when a 30-day implied-volatility index can be observed?
2. Can a long position in the **BVIV perpetual**, possibly timed by a **VWAP signal on 15-minute bars**, hedge **spot or perpetual BTC**, and what does that hedge cost?

The data are Binance BTCUSDT 15-minute bars, the official Volmex BVIV index and the Bitfinex perpetual's marks, order-book mids, open interest and every funding settlement, 2023-01 to 2026-10. The hedging rules are frozen on simulated training data, so the live sample is a clean out-of-sample test. A calibrated simulation with an arbitrage-free perpetual serves as a controlled experiment. One pipeline generates every table, figure and result in the paper.

1. **Forecasting.** HAR with implied variance is best at 1 day (QLIKE 0.96 × HAR, Diebold–Mariano t = −2.25) and 7 days (0.95 × HAR); the bias-corrected index is best at 30 days (0.84 × HAR). The simulation has the same winners.
2. **The hedge works, in crashes.** The minimum-variance hedge cut ES by 19.0% (95% block-bootstrap CI 6.9–33.3%) and the maximum drawdown from 53.0% to 41.0%. Net of funding, its ten best days earned 53% of BTC notional and all other days −46%.
3. **Funding is the price.** Bitfinex funding settled at its ±0.25% cap in 41% of 2,741 eight-hour periods; a permanently long contract paid 24 vol points a year (46 in 2024, 34 in 2025, −12 in 2026).
4. **Capacity is the limit.** The perpetual traded a median $71 a day; hedging one BTC needs a median 248 contracts against a median open interest of 84. On 4 October 2026 the book offered 1,667 contracts within 2.5% of the mid, the hedge of about 7 BTC.
5. **Timing adds size, not information.** The ratchet beat the static hedge by +1.94 pp of ES reduction (CI [−0.48, +4.08]) but beat only 50% of 194 placebos with the same triggers at shifted dates; a 1.5× static hedge did as well (20.5%). In the simulation (200 test paths) the ratchet beats the static hedge by +0.53 pp but its placebo by only +0.05 pp.
6. **Speed matters more than the signal.** Executing one hour late cut the ES reduction to 15.8%, one day late to 11.8%.

## How it works

```
 Binance 15m BTC ──► daily realised variance ──► HAR / HAR-IV / GARCH / EWMA / IV forecasts (logs)
        │                                              │ 30-day HAR-IV
        │                       Volmex BVIV index ─────┤
        │                                              ▼
        │                                     forecast "cheapness" ──► optional ratchet size m_t
        ▼
 rolling 24h VWAP ──► z = ln(S/VWAP) / (σ̂·√(1/3)) ──► breakdown trigger
                      σ̂: intraday EWMA of de-seasonalised 15m returns (half-life 1 day)
                                                          │
 Bitfinex BVIV perp (mark, mid, funding) ──────► EWMA MV hedge β, downside hedge ratio β⁻
                                                          ▼
                        VWAP ratchet: H = φ·H* + o_t·max(H⁻ − φ·H*, 0)
                        o_t = 1 on a breakdown, decays with half-life τ
                                                          ▼
          P&L engine: BTC leg (+ perp funding), index P&L, settled funding, premium, fees
```

* **Funding, reconstructed.** Bitfinex publishes the accrued premium of the perpetual over its mark once a minute. Settlements at 00/08/16 UTC follow `sign(a)·min(max(|a| − 0.05%, 0), 0.25%)` of the accrued premium `a`; the reconstruction matches the settled rates with a correlation of 0.985 (`src/bvivhedge/data.py`). Each settlement is charged to the position held at that moment (`src/bvivhedge/live.py`).
* **Honest fills.** Hedges trade at the perpetual's order-book mid plus fees and slippage, so buying at a premium to the mark is a cost. Positions move only when the target leaves a 25% band. Sensitivities cover execution one bar, one hour and one day late.
* **VWAP ratchet** (`src/bvivhedge/hedge.py`). The ratchet keeps a minimum-variance core hedge. When price breaks below the VWAP band, it raises protection to the downside hedge ratio straight away, then lets it decay slowly.
* **Frozen rules, single-path inference, placebos.** Rules are selected on 48 simulated training paths and never re-fitted on live data. Live intervals come from a paired stationary block bootstrap over days. Every ratchet is compared with copies whose trigger series is circularly shifted by whole weeks (same statistics, scrambled timing), which separates timing information from hedge size.
* **Controlled experiment** (`src/bvivhedge/simulate.py`). The simulated index is the model's exact 30-day variance expectation (Feynman–Kac over regime paths), and the perpetual's funding is the exact one-bar drift transfer plus a carry, so there is no free lunch from mean reversion. Both are checked against brute-force Monte Carlo in the tests.
* **No look-ahead.** Every signal and forecast is causal. Tests confirm that truncating the sample leaves past values of the VWAP signal, the hedge ratios and the regression forecasts unchanged.

## Reproduce

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python -m pytest                          # ~15 s
python scripts/fetch_data.py              # first run downloads ~1.3 million Bitfinex snapshots; cached afterwards
python scripts/run_live.py                # live study, ~5 min on 4 cores -> results/live/
python scripts/run_paper.py --stage paper # tables, figures, numbers and paper/main.pdf from cached results
python scripts/run_paper.py               # full simulation study, ~1 h on 4 cores, then the paper
python scripts/run_paper.py --quick       # smoke run of the simulation (8 paths), written to results/quick/
```

Building the PDF needs `latexmk` with a standard TeX distribution (TeX Live or TinyTeX). The live data come from public endpoints that need no key: `data.binance.vision` (BTC klines), `rest-v1.volmex.finance` (BVIV index), `api-pub.bitfinex.com` (perpetual status, candles, BTC-perp funding) and `www.deribit.com` (fallback BTC funding). `data.py` also has loaders for the Hyperliquid `mkts:BVIV` market (listed September 2026, too short for this study), Deribit DVOL and CSV exports of BVIV.

## Kurzfassung (Deutsch)

Seit April 2024 ist die implizite BTC-Volatilität handelbar: als Perpetual auf den Volmex-BVIV-Index, zuerst auf Bitfinex, inzwischen auch auf gTrade und Hyperliquid. Das Paper nutzt die vollständigen Bitfinex-Daten (Mark, Orderbuch-Mid, Open Interest, jede Funding-Abrechnung), den offiziellen Volmex-Index und Binance-BTC-Kurse auf 15-Minuten-Bars von 2023 bis Oktober 2026. Die Hedge-Regeln wurden vorab in einer Simulation festgelegt; die echten Daten sind damit ein reiner Out-of-Sample-Test.

1. **Volatilitätsprognose:** Für einen Tag ist HAR mit impliziter Varianz (HAR-IV) am besten und signifikant besser als HAR. Für sieben Tage liegt HAR-IV vorn, für 30 Tage der bias-korrigierte implizite Index; diese Unterschiede sind nicht signifikant.
2. **Der Hedge wirkt, und zwar im Crash:** Ein Minimum-Varianz-Hedge mit dem BVIV-Perp hat den Expected Shortfall eines BTC-Bestands um rund 19 % gesenkt (Konfidenzintervall etwa 7 bis 33 %) und den maximalen Drawdown von 53 % auf 41 %. Der Ertrag stammt aus wenigen Crash-Tagen, allen voran dem 5. Februar 2026, als der BVIV binnen Stunden von 57 auf 96 sprang.
3. **Funding ist der Preis:** Das Funding lag in rund 41 % der 8-Stunden-Perioden am Cap von ±0,25 %. Eine dauerhafte Long-Position hätte im Schnitt rund 24 Vola-Punkte pro Jahr gezahlt (2024: 46, 2025: 34, 2026: Longs haben erhalten).
4. **Kapazität ist die Grenze:** Der Perp handelte im Median rund 70 US-Dollar pro Tag, und schon der Hedge für einen einzigen BTC überstieg meist das gesamte Open Interest. Das Orderbuch ist tiefer als der Handel: Am 4. Oktober 2026 lagen innerhalb von 2,5 % um den Mid rund 1.700 Kontrakte, genug für den Hedge von etwa sieben BTC.
5. **VWAP-Timing bringt Größe, keine Information:** Der VWAP-Ratchet verbessert den Expected Shortfall um rund 2 Prozentpunkte, nicht signifikant. Placebos mit denselben Triggern zu verschobenen Zeitpunkten schaffen dasselbe, ebenso ein 1,5-fach skalierter statischer Hedge. Das Ein- und Ausschalten um den VWAP (Switch) schützt schlechter als der statische Hedge.
6. **Geschwindigkeit zählt mehr als das Signal:** Wird eine Stunde zu spät ausgeführt, sinkt die Shortfall-Reduktion von 19 % auf rund 16 %, bei einem Tag Verzögerung auf rund 12 %.

Die Simulation mit arbitragefrei bepreistem Perp und 200 Testpfaden bestätigt die Prognose-Rangfolge und das Timing-Ergebnis in kleinerem Maßstab.
